nauterm is a modern terminal application that replaces multiple tools. Use it to connect to remote servers, transfer files, manage serial devices, and keep your work organized — all from one clean interface. No programming knowledge required.

🔍 What is nauterm?

nauterm is a cross-platform remote access workspace. Think of it as a command center for your remote work. Instead of juggling between PuTTY, WinSCP, and a serial monitor, you get one application that handles SSH, SFTP, Telnet, serial connections, and more.
